Privacy policy

{{ site.name }} collects almost nothing about you, and sells nothing. Last updated 9 November 2026.

This website

  • No ads, no cookies, no accounts, no forms.
  • Visits are counted anonymously by GoatCounter: the page, the referring site and the device type. No IP addresses are kept and nothing follows you to other sites.
  • Your light or dark theme choice is stored only in your own browser.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Our Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and X accounts

When you follow, comment on or message our accounts, those platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies. We see what the platform shows any account owner (public comments, and totals such as reach and likes). We use those totals only to decide what to make next, and we never collect, sell or share personal information from them.

The Five Minute AI uploader (Google / YouTube)

The "Five Minute AI uploader" is our own tool. It signs in with Google only to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel (permission: manage your YouTube videos). It does not access, store or share anyone else's Google data. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can review YouTube's own handling of data in the Google Privacy Policy.
