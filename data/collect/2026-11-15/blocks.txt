Helen is a well-known figure in the AI safety and policy community, though she is perhaps best known for having served on the board of OpenAI, when she was one of four board members who voted to fire Sam Altman . That incident, now sometimes referred to as “the blip,” ended with Sam coming back as CEO, Helen resigning, and a new board of directors. OpenAI has since restructured entirely .
But it’s worth remembering what Helen and the board were supposed to do: OpenAI’s nonprofit board existed specifically to make sure the company didn’t betray its mission to build safe AI by prioritizing profit and growth over safety. When the board actually tried to pull the lever, that accountability mechanism broke, and here we are.

[S3] TechCrunch — "Where was Nvidia’s security team?" (15 November 2026)
Outsiders had little reason to notice when Nvidia’s longtime chief security officer and head of product security stepped down in August. Just two and a half months later, CEO Jensen Huang himself left the company over growing questions about his extramarital relationships — starting with an executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore data center company that U.S. investigators suspect of funneling Nvidia chips to China, then widening to reports about two separate affairs with Nvidia employees.
At the time, David Reber, who spent four years as CSO and seven at Nvidia overall, said he was leaving for health reasons. “This wasn’t a pivot I had planned. But I know what needs to come first: family and health,” he wrote on LinkedIn in late August, disclosing that he’d been diagnosed with young-onset Parkinson’s more than two years ago.
Reber’s replacement, Saša Zdjelar, previously spent 17 years at ExxonMobil in Houston, leaving as a senior IT manager before spending one year at Salesforce and several years at the cybersecurity company ReversingLabs. Zdjelar didn’t respond to questions Thursday about the transition or what the security team knew.
Nothing public connects Reber’s or Zdjelar’s role changes to the Megaspeed matter, but it seems fair to wonder what Nvidia’s security team knew about the government’s scrutiny of Megaspeed and about Huang’s relationships, and whether anyone raised concerns. Reber didn’t respond to questions Thursday. Nvidia also has not answered our related questions.
Relationships between a CEO and his employees typically fall to HR, legal and the board, not security. But security specialists say any affair — even one inside the office — can give a foreign intelligence service leverage over an exec who wants it kept quiet. And Huang’s relationship with an executive at Megaspeed, a company under federal investigation, wasn’t an office matter at all. (Megaspeed has denied any wrongdoing.)
Still, spotting the clues isn’t easy. The way intelligence services collect information, much of it through everyday conversation rather than hacking, makes it especially difficult for security teams to identify risks, according to retired senior intelligence officer Nicholas Eftimiades, who says he has trained more than 1,000 corporate security professionals.
Some people inside Nvidia clearly knew parts of the picture. Nvidia’s top lawyer was engaged with the government’s Megaspeed inquiry more than a year ago, The New York Times reported earlier this week . (Nvidia has said it regularly contacts officials.) [S5] The Verge — "Amazon has a plan to index the world’s online shopping data" (13 November 2026)
Amazon has a plan to index the world’s online shopping data
Listing the details of everything for sale everywhere is meant to make Amazon more useful than Reddit for people and AI agents.﻿
Amazon has plans to build a vault of data on every product sold anywhere on the internet, even products Amazon doesn’t sell, and make the information available through its own site, according to Reuters . The project, called the “World Product Graph,” is meant to make it less likely that shoppers leave Amazon to do their product research on Reddit or Google, and make it more likely shoppers end up at Amazon even if they’re searching for a product sold elsewhere. The same goes for AI agents — if Amazon has the largest stockpile of shopping data, AI agents may rely on its index of products over others, so even people shopping with bots will end up on its pages.
Links to outside retailers’ products have already started popping up on Amazon through the “shop direct” feature it launched last year. The company also has its own AI shopping assistant , Alexa for Shopping, previously called Rufus. The World Product Graph project would also be aimed at fixing issues with the AI by providing it more data. According to Reuters , an internal Amazon document on the project says “Many Rufus answers are factually wrong or incomplete” and running users’ Amazon searches with the AI is expensive. Adding more data is reportedly supposed to make the system more accurate and able to use cheaper models.

[S6] TechCrunch — "Elon Musk, other tech execs sign petition calling on Nvidia to reinstate Jensen Huang" (14 November 2026)
Elon Musk, other tech execs sign petition calling on Nvidia to reinstate Jensen Huang
A petition calling on Nvidia to reinstate the company’s recently ousted CEO Jensen Huang appears to have attracted more than 115,000 signatures as of Saturday afternoon.
The petition website said the signers include 5,965 current Nvidia employees, 1,108 former employees, and 63,063 shareholders. Notable supporters include Tesla and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, Replit CEO Amjad Masad, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, and Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan.
The petition states that its signers are “concerned that Jensen was removed without meaningful due process” and demands that the board “bring him back while an independent investigation establishes the facts.” It also calls on the board to ensure a fair investigation and provide more information to the public.
“Jensen deserves due process, not mob justice,” the petition says.
Huang resigned on Thursday , four days after confessing to having an affair with a chip executive suspected by U.S. intelligence to be a Chinese spy. The former CEO has agreed to testify before Congress, while news reports have also suggested that Huang engaged in multiple extramarital affairs .
Nvidia appointed CFO Colette Kress as the company’s interim CEO while it searches for a permanent replacement. Nvidia’s stock price fell sharply through the week, while Huang’s departure is also expected to leave a big gap in AI industry funding .
